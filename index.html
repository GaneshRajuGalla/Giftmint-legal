Giftmint

A private gift-card wallet for iPhone. Your cards, balances and expiry dates stay on your device — Giftmint has no accounts and no servers, and collects nothing about you.

Support

Found a bug, need help, or want to request a feature? Please open an issue and include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem occurred. Please do not include card numbers or PINs.

About your data

Everything the app has stored for you can be exported as a CSV from inside the app — that export is part of Giftmint Plus, and it never includes card numbers or PINs. You can also delete a card, or the app itself, to remove your data from your iPhone.

Giftmint is not affiliated with any retailer, issuer or gift-card network. Brand names belong to their owners.
