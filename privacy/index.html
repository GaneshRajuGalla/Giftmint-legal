Privacy Policy

Giftmint for iPhone · Effective 17 October 2026

Giftmint is a gift-card wallet that works entirely on your iPhone. We do not run a server that receives your information, we do not have accounts, and we do not collect analytics. This page explains exactly what the app stores, where it stores it, and what leaves your device — in plain language.

The short version. Your cards stay on your iPhone. We never see them.

1. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not show ads and we do not include advertising or tracking software.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not send your information to our own servers — we do not operate any.

The rest of this page describes the one exception category: information that stays on your iPhone, and the few occasions when Apple processes something on your behalf.

2. What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter or scan is stored in the app's private storage area on your iPhone:

  • Card details — brand, type, current balance, original value, expiry date, purchase state, notes, colour and a masked (partially hidden) card number, in the app's private on-device database.
  • Card number and PIN — held in the iOS Keychain, the system's encrypted credential store, marked as accessible only while the device is unlocked and only on this device. They are not stored in the app's database.
  • Photos you import — if you add a card by photograph, the image is stored in the app's private storage so you can view it later. Any text read from the image is read on your iPhone.
  • Store locations for nearby alerts — if you turn on nearby alerts, coordinates of relevant stores are stored on your iPhone so distances can be calculated.

3. Permissions, and what they are used for

Each permission is optional, is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings. You can use the wallet without granting any of them.

Camera

Used only to scan a gift card so you do not have to type its number. The image is processed on your iPhone. It is never uploaded.

Photos

If you import a picture of a card, the app reads the image you selected and processes it on your iPhone. The app does not browse or request access to your photo library.

Clipboard

If you tap “Paste from clipboard”, the text on your clipboard is read and parsed on your iPhone so the app can fill in the card's details. It is not uploaded. iOS may ask for your permission the first time.

Face ID / passcode

Used to protect your card numbers and PINs. Revealing a stored code, and changing a stored code, both require you to authenticate with Face ID or your device passcode. Your biometric data never leaves your device and is never available to the app — iOS only tells the app whether authentication succeeded.

Location

Used only if you turn on “Nearby alerts”. Giftmint uses your location on your iPhone to work out which of your saved cards can be spent at stores near you, and to alert you when you are close to one. Your location is not transmitted to us, because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to. If you never turn this on, the app never asks for your location. You can turn it off at any time in iOS Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Notifications

Expiry reminders and nearby alerts are scheduled locally on your iPhone. They are not sent through any notification server of ours.

4. Purchases

Giftmint Plus is an optional subscription sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, card number, billing address or any other payment detail. The app only asks Apple's StoreKit whether an active subscription exists. Apple's handling of your purchase is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

5. What leaves your device, and when

Giftmint itself does not transmit your data. Three things can involve Apple, and only these:

  • Nearby store lookups. If you enable nearby alerts, finding stores near you uses Apple's Maps services. Those requests go to Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and are for store locations — not for your card details.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple as described above.
  • Device backups. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, app data may be included in that backup. Backups are controlled by you and by Apple; we have no access to them.

Tapping a “Check balance online” link opens the relevant retailer's or issuer's own website in your browser. That website is not operated by us, and what it does with your visit is governed by its own privacy policy.

6. Children

Giftmint is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app collects nothing, there is nothing for us to delete on request — but if you believe a child has used the app, simply deleting the app's cards or the app itself removes the data from the device.

7. Your choices and control

  • Delete a card in the app and its stored number and PIN are deleted from the Keychain.
  • Delete the app and its on-device data is removed from your iPhone.
  • Turn off permissions at any time in iOS Settings; only the related feature stops working.
  • Export your data yourself with the app's CSV export (included with Giftmint Plus). The export contains your card details but never your card numbers or PINs.

Because we never receive your information, we hold nothing about you that we could access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. You are in complete control of it, on your device.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

9. Contact
